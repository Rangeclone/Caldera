<font size="18" color="#FFD966"><b>General</b></font>
<b>1.</b> Be respectful. No harassment, slurs, or targeting other players out of character.
<b>2.</b> No exploiting, glitching through walls, or abusing bugs. Report bugs to staff instead.
<b>3.</b> No spamming chat, radio or voice chat.
<b>4.</b> Follow the Roblox Terms of Use at all times.

<font size="18" color="#FFD966"><b>Roleplay</b></font>
<b>5.</b> Stay in character. Keep out-of-character talk short and use <b>//</b> before it.
<b>6.</b> No <font color="#FF6E6E"><b>RDM</b></font> (random deathmatch) with firearms on other inmates, unless defending yourself.
<b>7.</b> No <font color="#FF6E6E"><b>NITRP</b></font>, (no intention to rp).
<b>8.</b> No <font color="#FF6E6E"><b>FailRP</b></font>. Act like your situation is real: if you are cuffed at gunpoint, comply.
<b>9.</b> Inmates must follow the routine unless they are roleplaying a riot or escape.
<b>10.</b> No <font color="#FF6E6E"><b>NLR</b></font>, (new life rule) You cannot remember anything from your previous life, no meta gaming.

<font size="18" color="#FFD966"><b>Teams</b></font>
<b>11.</b> Do not join a team just to disrupt it or NITRP.
<b>12.</b> Do not use team tools or keycards for anything other than their purpose.
